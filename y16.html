Text 2

Text 2 原文 （错题：26、29、30；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1For the first time in history more people live in towns than in the country. In Britain this has had a curious result. While polls show Britons rate “the countryside” alongside the royal family, Shakespeare and the National Health Service (NHS) as what makes them proudest of their country, this has limited political support.

P2A century ago Octavia Hill launched the National Trust not to rescue stylish houses but to save “the beauty of natural places for everyone forever.” It was specifically to provide city dwellers with spaces for leisure where they could experience “a refreshing air.” Hill’s pressure later led to the creation of national parks and green belts. They don’t make countryside any more, and every year concrete consumes more of it. It needs constant guardianship.

P3At the next election none of the big parties seem likely to endorse this sentiment. The Conservatives' planning reform explicitly gives rural development priority over conservation, even authorising “off-plan” building where local people might object. The concept of sustainable development has been defined as profitable. Labour likewise wants to discontinue local planning where councils oppose development. The Liberal Democrats are silent. Only Ukip, sensing its chance, has sided with those pleading for a more considered approach to using green land. Its Campaign to Protect Rural England struck terror into many local Conservative parties.

P4The sensible place to build new houses, factories and offices is where people are, in cities and towns where infrastructure is in place. The London agents Stirling Ackroyd recently identified enough sites for half a million houses in the London area alone, with no intrusion on green belt. What is true of London is even truer of the provinces.

P5The idea that “housing crisis” equals “concreted meadows” is pure lobby talk. The issue is not the need for more houses but, as always, where to put them. Under lobby pressure, George Osborne favours rural new-build against urban renovation and renewal. He favours out-of-town shopping sites against high streets. This is not a free market but a biased one. Rural towns and villages have grown and will always grow. They do so best where building sticks to their edges and respects their character. We do not ruin urban conservation areas. Why ruin rural ones?

P6Development should be planned, not let rip. After the Netherlands, Britain is Europe's most crowded country. Half a century of town and country planning has enabled it to retain an enviable rural coherence, while still permitting low-density urban living. There is no doubt of the alternative—the corrupted landscapes of southern Portugal, Spain or Ireland. Avoiding this rather than promoting it should unite the left and right of the political spectrum.

第 26 题细节题

Britain's public sentiment about the countryside ____.

  1. Ahas brought much benefit to the NHS
  2. Bdidn't start till the Shakespearean age
  3. Cis fully backed by the royal family
  4. Dis not well reflected in politics
答案与解析

答案：D细节题

定位原文

P1While polls show Britons rate “the countryside” alongside the royal family, Shakespeare and the National Health Service (NHS) as what makes them proudest of their country, this has limited political support.

P3At the next election none of the big parties seem likely to endorse this sentiment.

解题思路

P1 末：英国人对乡村的自豪感“只得到有限的政治支持”；P3 进一步说各大党都不太可能支持这种情感——公众情感在政治中没有得到很好的体现。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
this has limited political support→is not well reflected in politics
干扰项陷阱
  • A张冠李戴NHS 只是与乡村并列的自豪对象，原文没说情感给它带来好处
  • B张冠李戴Shakespeare 是并列的自豪对象，不是时间起点
  • C偷换概念royal family 同样是并列项，不是支持者
第 29 题推断题（态度）

The author holds that George Osborne's preference ____.

  1. Areveals a strong prejudice against urban areas
  2. Bshows his disregard for the character of rural areas
  3. Cstresses the necessity of easing the housing crisis
  4. Dhighlights his firm stand against lobby pressure
答案与解析

答案：B推断题（态度）

定位原文

P5Under lobby pressure, George Osborne favours rural new-build against urban renovation and renewal.

P5They do so best where building sticks to their edges and respects their character.

P5Why ruin rural ones?

解题思路

奥斯本偏爱在乡村新建而非城市翻新，作者批评这是“有偏向的市场”，接着说乡镇发展最好是在边缘建设、“尊重其特色”，最后反问“为何要毁掉乡村保护区？”——说明奥斯本无视乡村特色。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
favours rural new-build … respects their character … Why ruin rural ones→disregard for the character of rural areas
干扰项陷阱
  • A偷换概念biased 指市场偏向乡村开发，并非对城市地区有“强烈偏见”；作者关心的是乡村被毁
  • C正反颠倒作者认为“住房危机=水泥化草地”纯属游说说辞，问题不在缺房而在建在哪儿
  • D正反颠倒原文是 Under lobby pressure，他是屈从于游说压力
第 30 题推断题（态度）

In the last paragraph, the author shows his appreciation of ____.

  1. Athe size of population in Britain
  2. Bthe enviable urban lifestyle in Britain
  3. Cthe town-and-country planning in Britain
  4. Dthe political life in today's Britain
答案与解析

答案：C推断题（态度）

定位原文

P6Half a century of town and country planning has enabled it to retain an enviable rural coherence, while still permitting low-density urban living.

解题思路

末段：半个世纪的城乡规划使英国保持了令人羡慕的乡村协调——作者赞赏的是城乡规划。enviable 修饰的是 rural coherence，不是城市生活。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
town and country planning has enabled it to retain an enviable rural coherence→the town-and-country planning in Britain
干扰项陷阱
  • A偷换概念英国人口拥挤是背景事实，不是赞赏对象
  • B张冠李戴enviable 修饰 rural coherence；城市生活只是 low-density（低密度）
  • D无中生有作者希望左右派团结，但没有赞赏当前政治生活，前文还在批评各党

Text 3

Text 3 原文 （错题：33；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1"There is one and only one social responsibility of business," wrote Milton Friedman, a Nobel prize-winning economist, "That is, to use its resources and engage in activities designed to increase its profits." But even if you accept Friedman's premise and regard corporate social responsibility (CSR) policies as a waste of shareholders' money, things may not be absolutely clear-cut. New research suggests that CSR may create monetary value for companies—at least when they are prosecuted for corruption.

P2The largest firms in America and Britain together spend more than $15 billion a year on CSR, according to an estimate by EPG, a consulting firm. This could add value to their businesses in three ways. First, consumers may take CSR spending as a “signal” that a company’s products are of high quality. Second, customers may be willing to buy a company’s products as an indirect way to donate to the good causes it helps. And third, through a more diffuse “halo effect,” whereby its good deeds earn it greater consideration from consumers and others.

P3Previous studies on CSR have had trouble differentiating these effects because consumers can be affected by all three. A recent study attempts to separate them by looking at bribery prosecutions under America's Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA). It argues that since prosecutors do not consume a company's products as part of their investigations, they could be influenced only by the halo effect.

P4The study found that, among prosecuted firms, those with the most comprehensive CSR programmes tended to get more lenient penalties. Their analysis ruled out the possibility that it was firms' political influence, rather than their CSR stand, that accounted for the leniency: Companies that contributed more to political campaigns did not receive lower fines.

P5In all, the study concludes that whereas prosecutors should only evaluate a case based on its merits, they do seem to be influenced by a company's record in CSR. "We estimate that either eliminating a substantial labour-rights concern, such as child labour, or increasing corporate giving by about 20% results in fines that generally are 40% lower than the typical punishment for bribing foreign officials," says one researcher.

P6Researchers admit that their study does not answer the question of how much businesses ought to spend on CSR. Nor does it reveal how much companies are banking on the halo effect, rather than the other possible benefits, when they decide their do-gooding policies. But at least they have demonstrated that when companies get into trouble with the law, evidence of good character can win them a less costly punishment.

第 33 题词义题

The expression “more lenient” (Para. 4) is closest in meaning to ____.

  1. Aless controversial
  2. Bmore lasting
  3. Cmore effective
  4. Dless severe
答案与解析

答案：D词义题

定位原文

P4The study found that, among prosecuted firms, those with the most comprehensive CSR programmes tended to get more lenient penalties.

P4Their analysis ruled out the possibility that it was firms' political influence, rather than their CSR stand, that accounted for the leniency: Companies that contributed more to political campaigns did not receive lower fines.

解题思路

lenient 修饰 penalties，下句用 did not receive lower fines 复现，P5 也说罚款低 40%——“更宽大的”即惩罚更轻。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
more lenient penalties ↔ lower fines→less severe
干扰项陷阱
  • A无关项controversial（有争议）与处罚轻重无关
  • B无关项lasting（持久）不符合语境
  • C无关项effective（有效）不对应罚款更低

Text 4

Text 4 原文 （错题：36、39；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1There will eventually come a day when The New York Times ceases to publish stories on newsprint. Exactly when that day will be is a matter of debate. “Sometime in the future,” the paper’s publisher said back in 2010.

P2Nostalgia for ink on paper and the rustle of pages aside, there's plenty of incentive to ditch print. The infrastructure required to make a physical newspaper—printing presses, delivery trucks—isn't just expensive; it's excessive at a time when online-only competitors don't have the same set of financial constraints. Readers are migrating away from print anyway. And though print ad sales still dwarf their online and mobile counterparts, revenue from print is still declining.

P3Overhead may be high and circulation lower, but rushing to eliminate its print edition would be a mistake, says BuzzFeed CEO Jonah Peretti.

P4Peretti says the Times shouldn't waste time getting out of the print business, but only if they go about doing it the right way. "Figuring out a way to accelerate that transition would make sense for them," he said, "but if you discontinue it, you're going to have your most loyal customers really upset with you."

P5Sometimes that's worth making a change anyway. Peretti gives the example of Netflix discontinuing its DVD-mailing service to focus on streaming. "It was seen as a blunder," he said. The move turned out to be foresighted. And if Peretti were in charge at the Times? "I wouldn't pick a year to end print," he said. "I would raise prices and make it into more of a legacy product."

P6The most loyal customers would still get the product they favor, the idea goes, and they'd feel like they were helping sustain the quality of something they believe in. "So if you're overpaying for print, you could feel like you were helping," Peretti said. "Then increase it at a higher rate each year and essentially try to generate additional revenue." In other words, if you're going to make a print product, make it for the people who are already obsessed with it. Which may be what the Times is doing already. Getting the print edition seven days a week costs nearly $500 a year—more than twice as much as a digital-only subscription.

P7"It's a really hard thing to do and it's a tremendous luxury that BuzzFeed doesn't have a legacy business," Peretti remarked. "But we're going to have questions like that where we have things we're doing that don't make sense when the market changes and the world changes. In those situations, it's better to be more aggressive than less aggressive."

第 36 题细节题

The New York Times is considering ending its print edition partly due to ____.

  1. Athe increasing online ad sales
  2. Bthe pressure from its investors
  3. Cthe complaints from its readers
  4. Dthe high cost of operation
答案与解析

答案：D细节题

定位原文

P2The infrastructure required to make a physical newspaper—printing presses, delivery trucks—isn't just expensive; it's excessive at a time when online-only competitors don't have the same set of financial constraints.

解题思路

P2：印刷报纸所需的基础设施（印刷机、送货卡车）不仅昂贵而且多余——运营成本高。P3 的 Overhead may be high 再次复现。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
isn't just expensive … Overhead may be high→the high cost of operation
干扰项陷阱
  • A正反颠倒原文是纸媒广告收入仍远超线上，但在下降；不是线上广告增加导致停刊
  • B无中生有投资者压力未提及
  • C正反颠倒读者是“流失”而非投诉；停印反而会让忠实读者不满
第 39 题细节题（观点）

Peretti believes that, in a changing world, ____.

  1. Atraditional luxuries can stay unaffected
  2. Bcautiousness facilitates problem-solving
  3. Caggressiveness better meets challenges
  4. Dlegacy businesses are becoming outdated
答案与解析

答案：C细节题（观点）

定位原文

P7In those situations, it's better to be more aggressive than less aggressive."

解题思路

末句：当市场和世界变化时，“更激进比不激进更好”——激进能更好应对挑战。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
when the market changes and the world changes … better to be more aggressive→in a changing world, aggressiveness better meets challenges
干扰项陷阱
  • A偷换概念luxury 指 BuzzFeed 没有传统业务是一种“幸运”，不是传统奢侈品不受影响
  • B正反颠倒作者主张激进而非谨慎
  • D过度推断Peretti 建议把纸质版做成 legacy product 提价经营，没说传统业务过时
