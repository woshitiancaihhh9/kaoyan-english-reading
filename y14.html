Text 2

Text 2 原文 （错题：26、27；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1All around the world, lawyers generate more hostility than the members of any other profession—with the possible exception of journalism. But there are few places where clients have more grounds for complaint than America.

P2During the decade before the economic crisis, spending on legal services in America grew twice as fast as inflation. The best lawyers made skyscrapers-full of money, tempting ever more students to pile into law schools. But most law graduates never get a big-firm job. Many of them instead become the kind of nuisance-lawsuit filer that makes the tort system a costly nightmare.

P3There are many reasons for this. One is the excessive costs of a legal education. There is just one path for a lawyer in most American states: a four-year undergraduate degree in some unrelated subject, then a three-year law degree at one of 200 law schools authorized by the American Bar Association and an expensive preparation for the bar exam. This leaves today's average law-school graduate with $100,000 of debt on top of undergraduate debts. Law-school debt means that they have to work fearsomely hard.

P4Reforming the system would help both lawyers and their customers. Sensible ideas have been around for a long time, but the state-level bodies that govern the profession have been too conservative to implement them. One idea is to allow people to study law as an undergraduate degree. Another is to let students sit for the bar after only two years of law school. If the bar exam is truly a stern enough test for a would-be lawyer, those who can sit it earlier should be allowed to do so. Students who do not need the extra training could cut their debt mountain by a third.

P5The other reason why costs are so high is the restrictive guild-like ownership structure of the business. Except in the District of Columbia, non-lawyers may not own any share of a law firm. This keeps fees high and innovation slow. There is pressure for change from within the profession, but opponents of change among the regulators insist that keeping outsiders out of a law firm isolates lawyers from the pressure to make money rather than serve clients ethically.

P6In fact, allowing non-lawyers to own shares in law firms would reduce costs and improve services to customers, by encouraging law firms to use technology and to employ professional managers to focus on improving firms' efficiency. After all, other countries, such as Australia and Britain, have started liberalizing their legal professions. America should follow.

第 26 题细节题（因果）

A lot of students take up law as their profession due to ____.

  1. Athe growing demand from clients
  2. Bthe increasing pressure of inflation
  3. Cthe prospect of working in big firms
  4. Dthe attraction of financial rewards
答案与解析

答案：D细节题（因果）

定位原文

P2The best lawyers made skyscrapers-full of money, tempting ever more students to pile into law schools.

解题思路

tempting 引出因果：顶尖律师赚得盆满钵满，吸引越来越多学生涌入法学院——即金钱回报的吸引力。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
made skyscrapers-full of money, tempting→the attraction of financial rewards
干扰项陷阱
  • A偷换概念法律服务支出增长≠客户需求增长导致学生学法
  • B张冠李戴inflation 只是比较对象（支出增速是通胀的两倍），不是原因
  • C正反颠倒原文紧接着说大多数毕业生根本进不了大所（never get a big-firm job）
第 27 题细节题

Which of the following adds to the costs of legal education in most American states?

  1. AHigher tuition fees for undergraduate studies.
  2. BPursuing a bachelor's degree in another major.
  3. CAdmissions approval from the bar association.
  4. DReceiving training by professional associations.
答案与解析

答案：B细节题

定位原文

P3There is just one path for a lawyer in most American states: a four-year undergraduate degree in some unrelated subject, then a three-year law degree at one of 200 law schools authorized by the American Bar Association and an expensive preparation for the bar exam.

解题思路

教育成本高的原因：先读 4 年“无关专业”本科，再读 3 年法学学位——必须先拿一个其他专业的学士学位，拉长了学制、增加了成本。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
a four-year undergraduate degree in some unrelated subject→Pursuing a bachelor's degree in another major
干扰项陷阱
  • A无中生有原文没有说本科学费更高
  • C偷换概念律协是“授权法学院”，不是对学生的入学审批
  • D偷换概念原文是昂贵的“律师资格考试备考”，不是专业协会的培训

Text 3

Text 3 原文 （错题：33、34；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1The US $3-million Fundamental Physics Prize is indeed an interesting experiment, as Alexander Polyakov said when he accepted this year's award in March. And it is far from the only one of its type. As a News Feature article in Nature discusses, a string of lucrative awards for researchers have joined the Nobel Prizes in recent years. Many, like the Fundamental Physics Prize, are funded from the telephone-number-sized bank accounts of Internet entrepreneurs. These benefactors have succeeded in their chosen fields, they say, and they want to use their wealth to draw attention to those who have succeeded in science.

P2What's not to like? Quite a lot, according to a handful of scientists quoted in the News Feature. You cannot buy class, as the old saying goes, and these upstart entrepreneurs cannot buy their prizes the prestige of the Nobels. The new awards are an exercise in self-promotion for those behind them, say scientists. They could distort the achievement-based system of peer-review-led research. They could cement the status quo of peer-reviewed research. They do not fund peer-reviewed research. They perpetuate the myth of the lone genius.

P3The goals of the prize-givers seem as scattered as the criticism. Some want to shock, others to draw people into science, or to better reward those who have made their careers in research.

P4As Nature has pointed out before, there are some legitimate concerns about how science prizes—both new and old—are distributed. The Breakthrough Prize in Life Sciences, launched this year, takes an unrepresentative view of what the life sciences include. But the Nobel Foundation’s limit of three recipients per prize, each of whom must still be living, has long been outgrown by the collaborative nature of modern research—as will be demonstrated by the inevitable row over who is ignored when it comes to acknowledging the discovery of the Higgs boson. The Nobels were, of course, themselves set up by a very rich individual who had decided what he wanted to do with his own money. Time, rather than intention, has given them legitimacy.

P5As much as some scientists may complain about the new awards, two things seem clear. First, most researchers would accept such a prize if they were offered one. Second, it is surely a good thing that the money and attention come to science rather than go elsewhere. It is fair to criticize and question the mechanism—that is the culture of research, after all—but it is the prize-givers' money to do with as they please. It is wise to take such gifts with gratitude and grace.

第 33 题细节题（例证）

The discovery of the Higgs boson is a typical case which involves ____.

  1. Athe joint effort of modern researchers
  2. Bcontroversies over the recipients' status
  3. Cthe demonstration of research findings
  4. Dlegitimate concerns over the new prizes
答案与解析

答案：B细节题（例证）

定位原文

P4But the Nobel Foundation’s limit of three recipients per prize, each of whom must still be living, has long been outgrown by the collaborative nature of modern research—as will be demonstrated by the inevitable row over who is ignored when it comes to acknowledging the discovery of the Higgs boson.

解题思路

希格斯玻色子用来证明诺奖“每奖最多 3 人且须在世”的规定已过时——必然会出现“谁被忽略”的争吵，即围绕获奖者资格的争议。例证题要看它证明的是哪句话。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
the inevitable row over who is ignored→controversies over the recipients' status
干扰项陷阱
  • A似是而非collaborative nature of modern research 是背景，例子要证明的是由此引起的“获奖人争议”
  • C偷换概念demonstrated 是“证明”诺奖规则过时，不是研究成果的展示
  • D张冠李戴希格斯玻色子针对的是诺贝尔奖（旧奖），不是新奖
第 34 题推断题（段落）

According to Paragraph 4, which of the following is true of the Nobels?

  1. AHistory has never cast doubt on them.
  2. BThey are the most representative honor.
  3. CTheir legitimacy has long been in dispute.
  4. DTheir endurance has done justice to them.
答案与解析

答案：D推断题（段落）

定位原文

P4Time, rather than intention, has given them legitimacy.

解题思路

段末：诺奖本身也是富人用自己的钱设立的，是“时间而非初衷”赋予了它合法性——它经久不衰才获得公正的认可。endurance ↔ Time，done justice ↔ given legitimacy。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
Time, rather than intention, has given them legitimacy→Their endurance has done justice to them
干扰项陷阱
  • A过于绝对本段恰恰质疑了诺奖“三人且在世”的规定，never cast doubt 太绝对
  • B张冠李戴unrepresentative 说的是生命科学突破奖，不是诺奖
  • C正反颠倒诺奖的合法性已经由时间确立，没说长期有争议

Text 4

Text 4 原文 （错题：38、39；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1"The Heart of the Matter," the just-released report by the American Academy of Arts and Sciences (AAAS), deserves praise for affirming the importance of the humanities and social sciences to the prosperity and security of liberal democracy in America. Regrettably, however, the report's failure to address the true nature of the crisis facing liberal education may cause more harm than good.

P2In 2010, leading congressional Democrats and Republicans sent letters to the AAAS asking that it identify actions that could be taken by “federal, state and local governments, universities, foundations, educators, individual benefactors and others” to “maintain national excellence in humanities and social scientific scholarship and education.” In response, the American Academy formed the Commission on the Humanities and Social Sciences. Among the commission’s 51 members are top-tier-university presidents, scholars, lawyers, judges, and business executives, as well as prominent figures from diplomacy, filmmaking, music and journalism.

P3The goals identified in the report are generally admirable. Because representative government presupposes an informed citizenry, the report supports full literacy; stresses the study of history and government, particularly American history and American government; and encourages the use of new digital technologies. To encourage innovation and competition, the report calls for increased investment in research, the crafting of coherent curricula that improve students' ability to solve problems and communicate effectively in the 21st century, increased funding for teachers and the encouragement of scholars to bring their learning to bear on the great challenges of the day. The report also advocates greater study of foreign languages, international affairs and the expansion of study abroad programs.

P4Unfortunately, despite 2½ years in the making, “The Heart of the Matter” never gets to the heart of the matter: the illiberal nature of liberal education at our leading colleges and universities. The commission ignores that for several decades America’s colleges and universities have produced graduates who don’t know the content and character of liberal education and are thus deprived of its benefits. Sadly, the spirit of inquiry once at home on campus has been replaced by the use of the humanities and social sciences as vehicles for publicizing “progressive,” or left-liberal propaganda.

P5Today, professors routinely treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy as the proper subject of study while portraying conservative or classical liberal ideas—such as free markets and self-reliance—as falling outside the boundaries of routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation.

P6The AAAS displays great enthusiasm for liberal education. Yet its report may well set back reform by obscuring the depth and breadth of the challenge that Congress asked it to illuminate.

第 38 题细节题（段落）

According to Paragraph 3, the report suggests ____.

  1. Aan exclusive study of American history
  2. Ba greater emphasis on theoretical subjects
  3. Cthe application of emerging technologies
  4. Dfunding for the study of foreign languages
答案与解析

答案：C细节题（段落）

定位原文

P3Because representative government presupposes an informed citizenry, the report supports full literacy; stresses the study of history and government, particularly American history and American government; and encourages the use of new digital technologies.

解题思路

P3 列举报告建议：……并鼓励使用新的数字技术。new digital technologies 同义改写为 emerging technologies。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
encourages the use of new digital technologies→the application of emerging technologies
干扰项陷阱
  • A过于绝对原文是 particularly（尤其）美国历史，不是 exclusive（唯一）
  • B无中生有原文强调解决问题和有效沟通的能力，没说强调理论学科
  • D张冠李戴funding 对应的是 teachers（增加教师经费）；外语只说 greater study（加强学习）
第 39 题推断题（态度）

The author implies in Paragraph 5 that professors are ____.

  1. Asupportive of free markets
  2. Bbiased against classical liberal ideas
  3. Ccautious about intellectual investigation
  4. Dconservative about public policy
答案与解析

答案：B推断题（态度）

定位原文

P5Today, professors routinely treat the progressive interpretation of history and progressive public policy as the proper subject of study while portraying conservative or classical liberal ideas—such as free markets and self-reliance—as falling outside the boundaries of routine, and sometimes legitimate, intellectual investigation.

解题思路

P5：教授们把进步主义视为正当研究对象，却把保守主义或古典自由主义观点描述为“超出正常（甚至合法）学术研究范畴”——说明他们对古典自由主义有偏见。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
portraying … classical liberal ideas … as falling outside the boundaries→biased against classical liberal ideas
干扰项陷阱
  • A正反颠倒自由市场正是被教授们排斥的观点
  • C偷换概念教授们不是对学术研究谨慎，而是有选择地排斥某些观点
  • D正反颠倒教授们推崇的是“进步主义”公共政策，不是保守
