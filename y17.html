Text 1

Text 1 原文 （错题：22、25；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1First two hours, now three hours—this is how far in advance authorities are recommending people show up to catch a domestic flight, at least at some major U.S. airports with increasingly massive security lines.

P2Americans are willing to tolerate time-consuming security procedures in return for increased safety. The crash of EgyptAir Flight 804, which terrorists may have downed over the Mediterranean Sea, provides another tragic reminder of why. But demanding too much of air travelers or providing too little security in return undermines public support for the process. And it should: Wasted time is a drag on Americans' economic and private lives, not to mention infuriating.

P3Last year, the Transportation Security Administration (TSA) found in a secret check that undercover investigators were able to sneak weapons—both fake and real—past airport security nearly every time they tried. Enhanced security measures since then, combined with a rise in airline travel due to the improving economy and low oil prices, have resulted in long waits at major airports such as Chicago’s O’Hare International. It is not yet clear how much more effective airline security has become—but the lines are obvious.

P4Part of the issue is that the government did not anticipate the steep increase in airline travel, so the TSA is now rushing to get new screeners on the line. Part of the issue is that airports have only so much room for screening lanes. Another factor may be that more people are trying to overpack their carry-on bags to avoid checked-baggage fees, though the airlines strongly dispute this.

P5There is one step the TSA could take that would not require remodeling airports or rushing to hire: Enroll more people in the PreCheck program. PreCheck is supposed to be a win-win for travelers and the TSA. Passengers who pass a background check are eligible to use expedited screening lanes. This allows the TSA to focus on travelers who are higher risk, saving time for everyone involved. The TSA wants to enroll 25 million people in PreCheck.

P6It has not gotten anywhere close to that, and one big reason is sticker shock: Passengers must pay $85 every five years to process their background checks. Since the beginning, this price tag has been PreCheck's fatal flaw. Upcoming reforms might bring the price to a more reasonable level. But Congress should look into doing so directly, by helping to finance PreCheck enrollment or to cut costs in other ways.

P7The TSA cannot continue diverting resources into underused PreCheck lanes while most of the traveling public suffers in unnecessary lines. It is long past time to make the program work.

第 22 题细节题（原因）

Which of the following contributes to long waits at major airports?

  1. ANew restrictions on carry-on bags.
  2. BThe declining efficiency of the TSA.
  3. CAn increase in the number of travelers.
  4. DFrequent unexpected secret checks.
答案与解析

答案：C细节题（原因）

定位原文

P3Enhanced security measures since then, combined with a rise in airline travel due to the improving economy and low oil prices, have resulted in long waits at major airports such as Chicago’s O’Hare International.

P4Part of the issue is that the government did not anticipate the steep increase in airline travel, so the TSA is now rushing to get new screeners on the line.

解题思路

P3：加强的安检措施加上航空出行人数增加，导致大机场排长队；P4 再次说政府没预料到航空出行的急剧增长。rise in airline travel = increase in the number of travelers。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
a rise in airline travel … have resulted in long waits→An increase in the number of travelers
干扰项陷阱
  • A无中生有原文是乘客为避免托运费而把随身行李塞得太满（且航空公司否认），并非“新的随身行李限制”
  • B无中生有原文说安检是否更有效尚不清楚，没说 TSA 效率下降
  • D张冠李戴秘密检查是去年的一次调查，是加强安检的起因，不是排队的直接原因，frequent 也无依据
第 25 题主旨题

Which of the following would be the best title for the text?

  1. AGetting Stuck in Security Lines
  2. BPreCheck—a Belated Solution
  3. CLess Screening for More Safety
  4. DUnderused PreCheck Lanes
答案与解析

答案：A主旨题

定位原文

P1First two hours, now three hours—this is how far in advance authorities are recommending people show up to catch a domestic flight, at least at some major U.S. airports with increasingly massive security lines.

P7It is long past time to make the program work.

解题思路

全文结构：开头提出问题（安检队伍越来越长）→ 分析原因 → 提出办法（扩大 PreCheck）→ 分析 PreCheck 的问题。核心话题始终是“被困在安检长队中”，PreCheck 只是解决方案的一部分。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
increasingly massive security lines … long waits→Getting Stuck in Security Lines
干扰项陷阱
  • B以偏概全+无中生有PreCheck 只是解决方案，且 belated（迟来的）无依据——它早已存在只是没用好
  • C正反颠倒作者并不主张减少安检，PreCheck 是让低风险乘客快速通过
  • D以偏概全underused PreCheck lanes 只出现在末段，是细节

Text 2

Text 2 原文 （错题：28、30；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1"The ancient Hawaiians were astronomers," wrote Queen Liliuokalani, Hawaii's last reigning monarch, in 1897. Star watchers were among the most esteemed members of Hawaiian society. Sadly, all is not well with astronomy in Hawaii today. Protests have erupted over construction of the Thirty Meter Telescope (TMT), a giant observatory that promises to revolutionize humanity's view of the cosmos.

P2At issue is the TMT's planned location on Mauna Kea, a dormant volcano worshiped by some Hawaiians as the piko, that connects the Hawaiian Islands to the heavens. But Mauna Kea is also home to some of the world's most powerful telescopes. Rested in the Pacific Ocean, Mauna Kea's peak rises above the bulk of our planet's dense atmosphere, where conditions allow telescopes to obtain images of unsurpassed clarity.

P3Opposition to telescopes on Mauna Kea is nothing new. A small but vocal group of Hawaiians and environmentalists have long viewed their presence as disrespect for sacred land and a painful reminder of the occupation of what was once a sovereign nation.

P4Some blame for the current controversy belongs to astronomers. In their eagerness to build bigger telescopes, they forgot that science is not the only way of understanding the world. They did not always prioritize the protection of Mauna Kea's fragile ecosystems or its holiness to the islands' inhabitants. Hawaiian culture is not a relic of the past; it is a living culture undergoing a renaissance today.

P5Yet science has a cultural history, too, with roots going back to the dawn of civilization. The same curiosity to find what lies beyond the horizon that first brought early Polynesians to Hawaii's shores inspires astronomers today to explore the heavens. Calls to disassemble all telescopes on Mauna Kea or to ban future development there ignore the reality that astronomy and Hawaiian culture both seek to answer big questions about who we are, where we come from and where we are going. Perhaps that is why we explore the starry skies, as if answering a primal calling to know ourselves and our true ancestral homes.

P6The astronomy community is making compromises to change its use of Mauna Kea. The TMT site was chosen to minimize the telescope's visibility around the island and to avoid archaeological and environmental impact. To limit the number of telescopes on Mauna Kea, old ones will be removed at the end of their lifetimes and their sites returned to a natural state. There is no reason why everyone cannot be welcomed on Mauna Kea to embrace their cultural heritage and to study the stars.

第 28 题细节题（原因）

The construction of the TMT is opposed by some locals partly because ____.

  1. Ait may risk ruining their intellectual life
  2. Bthey fear losing control of Mauna Kea
  3. Ctheir culture will lose a chance of revival
  4. Dit reminds them of a humiliating history
答案与解析

答案：D细节题（原因）

定位原文

P3A small but vocal group of Hawaiians and environmentalists have long viewed their presence as disrespect for sacred land and a painful reminder of the occupation of what was once a sovereign nation.

解题思路

P3：反对者认为望远镜是对圣地的不尊重，也让他们痛苦地想起这片曾经的主权国家被占领的历史——即屈辱的历史。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
a painful reminder of the occupation→reminds them of a humiliating history
干扰项陷阱
  • A无中生有原文没有提“知识生活”
  • B过度推断原文是对圣地的不尊重，不是害怕失去控制权
  • C正反颠倒原文说夏威夷文化“正在复兴”（undergoing a renaissance），并非将失去复兴机会
第 30 题态度题

The author's attitude toward choosing Mauna Kea as the TMT site is one of ____.

  1. Asevere criticism
  2. Bfull approval
  3. Cpassive acceptance
  4. Dslight hesitancy
答案与解析

答案：B态度题

定位原文

P6The TMT site was chosen to minimize the telescope's visibility around the island and to avoid archaeological and environmental impact.

P6There is no reason why everyone cannot be welcomed on Mauna Kea to embrace their cultural heritage and to study the stars.

解题思路

作者虽承认天文学家有过失（P4），但 P5 用 Yet 转折为天文学辩护，P6 说 TMT 选址已尽量减少影响、天文界在做出妥协，结尾说“没有理由不让所有人都在山上……研究星空”——作者完全赞同选址。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
There is no reason why everyone cannot be welcomed … to study the stars→full approval
干扰项陷阱
  • A正反颠倒作者批评的是天文学家过去不够重视生态和圣地，不是选址
  • C程度偏差作者主动为天文学辩护，不是“被动接受”
  • D程度偏差结尾语气坚定（There is no reason why…），没有犹豫

Text 3

Text 3 原文 （错题：32；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1Robert F. Kennedy once said that a country's GDP measures “everything except that which makes life worthwhile.” With Britain voting to leave the European Union, and GDP already predicted to slow as a result, it is now a timely moment to assess what he was referring to.

P2The question of GDP and its usefulness has annoyed policymakers for over half a century. Many argue that it is a flawed concept. It measures things that do not matter and misses things that do. By most recent measures, the UK's GDP has been the envy of the Western world, with record low unemployment and high growth figures. If everything was going so well, then why did over 17 million people vote for Brexit, despite the warnings about what it could do to their country's economic prospects?

P3A recent annual study of countries and their ability to convert growth into well-being sheds some light on that question. Across the 163 countries measured, the UK is one of the poorest performers in ensuring that economic growth is translated into meaningful improvements for its citizens. Rather than just focusing on GDP, over 40 different sets of criteria from health, education and civil society engagement have been measured to get a more rounded assessment of how countries are performing.

P4While all of these countries face their own challenges, there are a number of consistent themes. Yes, there has been a budding economic recovery since the 2008 global crash, but in key indicators in areas such as health and education, major economies have continued to decline. Yet this isn't the case with all countries. Some relatively poor European countries have seen huge improvements across measures including civil society, income equality and the environment.

P5This is a lesson that rich countries can learn: When GDP is no longer regarded as the sole measure of a country's success, the world looks very different.

P6So, what Kennedy was referring to was that while GDP has been the most common method for measuring the economic activity of nations, as a measure, it is no longer enough. It does not include important factors such as environmental quality or education outcomes—all things that contribute to a person’s sense of well-being.

P7The sharp hit to growth predicted around the world and in the UK could lead to a decline in the everyday services we depend on for our well-being and for growth. But policymakers who refocus efforts on improving well-being rather than simply worrying about GDP figures could avoid the forecasted doom and may even see progress.

第 32 题推断题（段落）

It can be inferred from Paragraph 2 that ____.

  1. AGDP as the measure of success is widely defied in the UK
  2. Bpolicymakers in the UK are paying less attention to GDP
  3. Cthe UK will contribute less to the world economy
  4. Dthe UK is reluctant to remold its economic pattern
答案与解析

答案：A推断题（段落）

定位原文

P2Many argue that it is a flawed concept.

P2If everything was going so well, then why did over 17 million people vote for Brexit, despite the warnings about what it could do to their country's economic prospects?

解题思路

P2：许多人认为 GDP 是有缺陷的概念；英国 GDP 表现令西方羡慕，却有 1700 多万人投票脱欧——说明人们并不认同“GDP 好=国家成功”，以 GDP 作为成功的衡量标准在英国受到广泛质疑。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
a flawed concept … over 17 million people vote for Brexit→GDP as the measure of success is widely defied
干扰项陷阱
  • B张冠李戴原文说 GDP 问题困扰决策者，没说他们更少关注 GDP；末段反而建议他们别只盯着 GDP
  • C无中生有英国对世界经济的贡献未提及
  • D无中生有英国是否愿意重塑经济模式未提及

Text 4

Text 4 原文 （错题：36、37、39；答完后对应定位句会在原文中高亮）

P1In a rare unanimous ruling, the US Supreme Court has overturned the corruption conviction of a former Virginia governor, Robert McDonnell. But it did so while holding its nose at the ethics of his conduct, which included accepting gifts such as a Rolex watch and a Ferrari automobile from a company seeking access to government.

P2The high court's decision said the judge in Mr. McDonnell's trial failed to tell a jury that it must look only at his “official acts,” or the former governor's decisions on “specific” and “unsettled” issues related to his duties.

P3Merely helping a gift-giver gain access to other officials, unless done with clear intent to pressure those officials, is not corruption, the justices found.

P4The court did suggest that accepting favors in return for opening doors is “distasteful” and “nasty”. But under anti-bribery laws, proof must be made of concrete benefits, such as approval of a contract or regulation. Simply arranging a meeting, making a phone call, or hosting an event is not an “official act”.

P5The court's ruling is legally sound in defining a kind of favoritism that is not criminal. Elected leaders must be allowed to help supporters deal with bureaucratic problems without fear of prosecution for bribery. “The basic compact underlying representative government,” wrote Chief Justice John Roberts for the court, “assumes that public officials will hear from their constituents and act on their concerns.”

P6But the ruling reinforces the need for citizens and their elected representatives, not the courts, to ensure equality of access to government. Officials must not be allowed to play favorites in providing information or in arranging meetings simply because an individual or group provides a campaign donation or a personal gift. This type of integrity requires well-enforced laws in government transparency, such as records of official meetings, rules on lobbying, and information about each elected leader's source of wealth.

P7Favoritism in official access can fan public perceptions of corruption. But it is not always corruption. Rather officials must avoid double standards, or different types of access for average people and the wealthy. If connections can be bought, a basic premise of democratic society—that all are equal in treatment by government—is undermined. Good governance rests on an understanding of the inherent worth of each individual.

P8The court's ruling is a step forward in the struggle against both corruption and official favoritism.

第 36 题推断题（句子）

The underlined sentence (Para.1) most probably shows that the court ____.

  1. Amade no compromise in convicting McDonnell
  2. Bavoided defining the extent of McDonnell's duties
  3. Cwas contemptuous of McDonnell's conduct
  4. Drefused to comment on McDonnell's ethics
答案与解析

答案：C推断题（句子）

定位原文

P1But it did so while holding its nose at the ethics of his conduct, which included accepting gifts such as a Rolex watch and a Ferrari automobile from a company seeking access to government.

解题思路

holding its nose（捏着鼻子）表示嫌恶、鄙视。P4 的 “distasteful” and “nasty” 复现了这种态度——法院虽推翻定罪，但鄙视他的行为。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
holding its nose at the ethics of his conduct ↔ “distasteful” and “nasty”→was contemptuous of McDonnell's conduct
干扰项陷阱
  • A正反颠倒法院“推翻”了对他的定罪
  • B正反颠倒P2 法院恰恰界定了 official acts 的范围
  • D正反颠倒法院评论了他的道德（distasteful / nasty）
第 37 题细节题（段落）

According to Paragraph 4, an official act is deemed corruptive only if it involves ____.

  1. Aconcrete returns for gift-givers
  2. Bsizable gains in the form of gifts
  3. Cleaking secrets intentionally
  4. Dbreaking contracts officially
答案与解析

答案：A细节题（段落）

定位原文

P4But under anti-bribery laws, proof must be made of concrete benefits, such as approval of a contract or regulation.

解题思路

P4：根据反贿赂法，必须证明有具体的利益（如批准合同或法规）——即给送礼者带来具体回报。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
proof must be made of concrete benefits→concrete returns for gift-givers
干扰项陷阱
  • B张冠李戴礼物（劳力士、法拉利）是官员得到的，判定腐败看的是送礼者获得的具体利益
  • C无中生有泄露机密未提及
  • D偷换概念原文是“批准合同”，不是“违反合同”
第 39 题细节题（目的）

Well-enforced laws in government transparency are needed to ____.

  1. Aawaken the conscience of officials
  2. Ballow for certain kinds of lobbying
  3. Cguarantee fair play in official access
  4. Dinspire hopes in average people
答案与解析

答案：C细节题（目的）

定位原文

P6But the ruling reinforces the need for citizens and their elected representatives, not the courts, to ensure equality of access to government.

P6This type of integrity requires well-enforced laws in government transparency, such as records of official meetings, rules on lobbying, and information about each elected leader's source of wealth.

解题思路

P6：要确保公民平等接触政府，官员不能因捐款或礼物而偏袒——这种正直需要执行良好的政府透明法律。目的就是保证官方接触中的公平。

同义改写对照
原文正确选项
ensure equality of access to government→guarantee fair play in official access
干扰项陷阱
  • A无中生有原文靠法律制度，不靠唤醒良知
  • B偷换概念rules on lobbying 是“规范游说”，不是“允许某些游说”
  • D无中生有给普通人希望未提及
